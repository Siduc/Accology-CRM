Step 1

What is Accologise?

Accologise is a CRM built for UK accountancy practices. It joins up clients, compliance jobs (Accounts, Self Assessment, Confirmation Statements), sales invoices, debtors, cash, and purchase bills so you can see the practice as a working-capital cycle — not separate spreadsheets.

  • One place for WIP, billing, and who owes what
  • Designed for day-to-day desk work (lists, status pins, Done buttons)
  • Phone-friendly for checking jobs and tasks on the move

Step 2

The working capital desk

From the home dashboard, the practice money story is:

  1. Work in progress — open jobs & retainers not yet billed
  2. Sales — raise invoices when work is done
  3. Debtors — unpaid client invoices (credit control / chase)
  4. Cash — bank & payments
  5. Creditors — supplier bills you need to pay

Prospecting (pipeline) and the task ledger sit alongside so nothing falls through the cracks.

Step 3

Jobs → Done → Invoice

Jobs are the core of compliance work. Filter by type (Accounts, SAR, Confirmation Statement) or by time band (Today, Tomorrow, This week).

  • Open a job to see dates, fee, status, and linked tasks
  • Press Done when work is finished — that marks the job complete, creates next year’s job if it is recurring, and opens the invoice
  • Invoices look like real invoices; you can edit number, lines, and dates

Step 4

Task ledger

Smaller pieces of work live on the task ledger (including email-style follow-ups). Use Done on a task the same way — complete and move on. Tasks can link to a client or job so context stays with the work.

Step 5

What you will see in the demo

Demo login is anonymised. Client and people names are replaced with sample names. Fees show polished demo amounts (e.g. Accounts £10,000 · Confirmation Statement £100 · SAR £1,000). The real practice database is not exposed and cannot be switched to “live” on a demo login.
  • Explore freely — navigation and workflows are real
  • Figures and names are for presentation, not live books
  • Exports of sensitive live data are blocked in demo
